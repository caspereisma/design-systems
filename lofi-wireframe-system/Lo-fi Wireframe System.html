01 / FOUNDATIONS
Why lo-fi

Sketchy on purpose.

Lo-fi wireframes invite feedback. Hi-fi mocks invite arguments about pixel padding. Use this kit when the conversation should be about structure, not polish.

①
Speed over polish. Get a layout on the page in minutes. Throw it away in seconds.
PRINCIPLE 01
②
Honest fidelity. A lo-fi sketch is obviously a sketch. Stakeholders don't argue about colors that aren't there.
PRINCIPLE 02
③
One blue. Reserve a single accent for interactive elements. Everything else is graphite.
PRINCIPLE 03
